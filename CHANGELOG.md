# Changelog

## 0.2.5, 2026-11-15

- Crew: posts with a speciality choice (Captain, Engineer, Gunner, Marine)
  can have more than one row, each with its own speciality, count and
  level. Reroll keeps the speciality and level the NPC was rolled for.

## 0.2.4, 2026-11-15

- Crew: the Engineer post can ask for one Engineer speciality (j-drive,
  m-drive, power or life support).

## 0.2.3, 2026-11-15

- Crew: the Gunner post can ask for a Gunner speciality (turret, ortillery,
  screen or capital).

## 0.2.2, 2026-11-15

- Crew: a captain can be filled by Leadership, Broker or Admin, or you can
  insist on one of them.

## 0.2.1, 2026-11-15

- Crew: the Marine post can ask for a Gun Combat speciality (archaic, energy
  or slug) instead of any Gun Combat or Melee.
- Starting equipment shows each item's skill and your level in it, and can
  list only what you have the skill for.

## 0.2.0, 2026-11-15

- Roll a ship's crew: the count and skill level wanted for each post
  (High Guard's crew table and the marine), or a Core ship's crew line to
  start from. Each post gets an NPC who can do the job, with salary,
  payroll, reroll and CSV, Markdown and text export.
- NPCs no longer always take the first speciality (Gun Combat was always
  archaic), the first two university skills, the first skills package or the
  dearest benefit item.

## 0.1.0, 2026-11-15

First version.

- Traveller creation to the Core Rulebook Update 2022: characteristics,
  background skills, university and the academies, the twelve careers,
  Prisoner, Psion, the draft and the Drifter, every event and mishap,
  commissions and advancement, ageing and anagathics, injury and medical
  bills, mustering out, pensions, skills packages and connections.
- Aslan and Vargr; the Glorious Empire careers for Aslan and human slaves.
- Benefits chosen from the Core equipment lists and the Central Supply
  Catalogue's augments; ships with quirks and mortgages.
- Starting equipment shop, printable sheet, text and Markdown export,
  `.traveller` save files, undo, resume.
- Quick NPCs and crowds with CSV export; a rules browser.
